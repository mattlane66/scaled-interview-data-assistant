# I03 — Cross-interview reuse

## S01 — Current struggle, turn 1

**RESEARCHER:** Where did the analysis become difficult?

**PARTICIPANT:** The hard part was not summarizing one interview; it was seeing patterns across all of
them. Individual summaries hid which situations were actually recurring.

## S02 — Desired progress, turn 2

**RESEARCHER:** Who needed to use the result?

**PARTICIPANT:** The output had to be reusable by the team, not just understandable to the person who
ran the interviews. They needed the evidence and the definitions, not only my conclusions.
