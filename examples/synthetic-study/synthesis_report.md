# Synthetic Study Synthesis

## Scope

- Interviews: I01–I03
- Decision episodes: D01–D03
- Review status: Synthetic evidence and mappings reviewed against source transcripts

## Method

- Analysis unit: Decision episode.
- Clustering method: Average-linkage hierarchical clustering on Jaccard distance.
- Computed vs. heuristic: Matrices, associations, and clusters computed; interpretations reviewed.
- Association note: Jaccard and phi describe code co-occurrence; this is not causal.
- Support limit: Three synthetic episodes are useful for demonstration, not generalization.

## High-Level Patterns

### VERIFIED

- Scattered quote handling can cause the analyst to lose context. E001
- Larger batches can make naming and synthesis inconsistent. E003
- Teams need evidence and definitions, not only conclusions. E006

### INFERRED

- Traceability, reusable codes, and reusable team outputs are distinct forms of continuity across a
  study rather than one generic request for automation. E002 E004 E006
- D02 and D03 share scale pressure, but D02 emphasizes code consistency while D03 emphasizes
  cross-interview and team reuse. E003 E004 E005 E006

## Implications

### VERIFIED

- Any workflow represented by this synthetic study must preserve source traceability. E002

### INFERRED
