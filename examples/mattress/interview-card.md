## Job Story

WHEN I keep waking sore and losing the first useful hour of the day,

AND replacement feels expensive, time-consuming, and difficult to reverse,

I WANT a credible, low-risk way to change the sleep setup,

SO I CAN wake functional and be more present in the morning.
