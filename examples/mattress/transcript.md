# Synthetic mattress interview

This is an original synthetic teaching transcript. It is not participant data and should not be
treated as evidence about a real market.

## S01 — Recognizing the struggle (00:00–03:20)

**INTERVIEWER:** Take me back to when replacing the mattress became a real problem rather than a
general thought.

**ALEX:** For most of the last year I woke up with a stiff back and felt tired before the day started.
The mattress was eight years old and had a dip on my side. I kept saying I would replace it after our
move, but after the move there was always something more urgent.

**INTERVIEWER:** What did that affect?

**ALEX:** I was slow in the morning and short with my partner. By the time I loosened up, I had already
lost the first useful hour of the day.

## S02 — Path Y and inertia (03:20–06:40)

**INTERVIEWER:** What did you try before buying something else?

**ALEX:** We rotated it, added a foam topper, and I tried sleeping on the couch twice. The topper helped
for maybe a week. I also looked at mattresses online, but the return process sounded like a project.

**INTERVIEWER:** Why did you keep waiting?

**ALEX:** We had just moved, money was going everywhere, and spending a Saturday in a mattress store
was the last thing either of us wanted. I worried we would spend a lot and make the same mistake.

## S03 — Tipping moment and Path X (06:40–10:15)

**INTERVIEWER:** What changed?

**ALEX:** I slept in a hotel on a work trip and woke up without the usual pain. That made it hard to keep
pretending the mattress was not the cause. The next weekend we saw boxed mattresses at the warehouse
store while buying shelves. The sign explained the return policy, the box fit in our car, and my
partner said, “If we can take it back here, let’s stop talking about it and do it.”

**INTERVIEWER:** Why was that different from the options you had researched?

**ALEX:** I did not have to deal with a salesperson or arrange delivery before knowing whether we
wanted it. If it failed, the store was fifteen minutes away. It felt reversible.

## S04 — Point B and outcome boundary (10:15–12:00)

**INTERVIEWER:** What did you hope would be different after the purchase?

**ALEX:** I wanted to wake up without negotiating with my back and have that first hour again. Mostly I
wanted to be useful and pleasant in the morning instead of starting every day behind.

**INTERVIEWER:** How has the new mattress performed?

**ALEX:** I cannot say yet. We bought it yesterday and it is still expanding. I can explain why we finally
acted, but not whether it solved the problem.
