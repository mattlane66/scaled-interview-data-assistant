# Next research decision — four methods, one canonical case

**Initial result, 2026-11-22:** `INVESTIGATE` with explicit **human/fieldwork gates**, not `COMPLETE`. Public-source SCOUT has begun and produced the [ledger](source-ledger.json); the full STANDARD/FULL von Hippel protocol and predictive validation **have not run**.

## Decision to inform

> Should the next mattress study concentrate on making an initial purchase reversible, making mattress evaluation more trustworthy, or letting households change and repair comfort *after* purchase—and for whom?

A positive result would refine the competing explanations and a viable *research* program, not select a product shape. The original Costco interview is one case; none of the three alternatives has been causally demonstrated.

### 2. Behavioral Mechanism Map — contrast actual decisions

- Recruit additional **consenting**, differently situated participants who bought, returned, retained, modified, and declined to buy (including genuine negative cases).
- Keep the units as bounded **decision episodes**; a repeated Reddit comment by one author is not an additional person.
- Investigate recovery anxiety versus seller trust, tactile evaluation versus co-decision, opportunity timing versus readiness, and reversible purchase versus replaceable components. Record a rival and falsifier for each.
- Apply mechanism-map review gates and run its deterministic compiler only after real human source and contrasting-case review; treat computed associations as descriptive.
- **Current status:** `EXPLORATORY_CROSS_SOURCE_DESK_CONTRASTS`, not reviewed map.

### 3. Behavioral Simulation + SSR — evidence and independent holdout

- Do not upgrade existing `L0_ROLEPLAY` variants. Preserve them as demonstrations.
- For one *precisely specified* intervention and target population, pre-register the ordinal question, anchors, elicitation, embedding/version and aggregate before generating any synthetic responses.
- Obtain a separate human sample of actual responses to the **same** question, with selection/recruitment record and no shared source leakage. Compare real and modeled distributions with appropriate uncertainty; also evaluate actual behavior separately if claiming purchase prediction.
- Report calibration/validation **only if** the holdout supports it. If not, retain the negative result, and avoid using SSR to choose a product.
- **Current status:** `NO_INDEPENDENT_HOLDOUT`, `NOT_VALIDATED`.

### 4. Lead User Research — qualify genuine advanced cases

- **Trend first:** investigate evidence for important changes in home evaluation, adaptive support or modular repair. The verified online-shopping survey trend alone **does not** prove modular DIY trend importance.
- **Discover/pyramid:** use the three public DIY writers as *discovery seeds*, not a complete search universe. Find maker communities, independent sleep-product engineers, support/repair practitioners, people experimenting outside bedding, and negative cases; record referral lineage and search bias.
- **Qualify LU1 and LU2 per bounded need episode:** evidence of operating ahead of the named *important trend* and unusually high expected benefit must be shown independently; technical expertise or many purchases are not substitutes.
- **Investigate episodes:** obtain direct interviews when permitted and real build notes/usage/repair traces; record alternatives, what failed, costs and observed outcomes. Test cross-context transfer and contradiction. Preserve self-report uncertainty.
- Run STANDARD phases B–D only after sufficient evidence and freeze; then E interpret, and F concept only if its gate passes. Import returned references as a separate `LUR:<workspace>` sidecar; **original x unchanged** until authorized human decision.
- **Current status:** `PUBLIC_DESK_SCOUT`; **zero qualified LU episodes**, zero firsthand interviews.

## Stop rules and acceptance

A source-grounded document is not equivalent to an independently validated finding. Stop or revise a hypothesis if opposing cases undermine it; if a qualified trend or LU1/LU2 evidence cannot be shown, report **NOT QUALIFIED**, don't stretch definitions. If the original transcript cannot establish an outcome, preserve **UNKNOWN**. If a simulation fails its holdout, report **NOT VALIDATED**.

The final canonical example should present for each method: its input cases, source receipts/locators, computation logs (if run), explicit human review history, rival explanations, negative cases, limits, and what it **uniquely** changed about the next decision. Never promote a research-local frame, mechanisms, SSR score, or lead-user transfer hypothesis into accepted product requirements.

## Corrective sampling priority

The desk sample systematically privileges English-language, technically confident people publishing unusual experiences online. Next discovery must include ordinary recent mattress buyers, people who elected *not* to switch, people who returned conventional mattresses, couples who disagreed, and independent repair practitioners. Actual consent and researcher contact are not implied or recorded.
